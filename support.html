Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app, so you cancel in your Apple account settings:

  • Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose FrostPass and tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. You can also open Settings inside the app and tap "Manage or cancel subscription".

I subscribed before, but the app asks me to subscribe again

Open the subscription screen or Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. For Apple, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases ourselves.

Why was I charged?

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. You can see your charges and renewal date in your subscription settings.

Using the app

I found a wrong or unclear question

Thank you for telling us. Tap the flag icon at the top of any question to send a report, and tell us what the correct answer should be. You can also email us.

Where is my progress saved? What if I change phones or delete the app?

Your answers, scores and streak are saved on your device only. They are not stored in an account. If you delete the app or switch to a new phone, your progress does not move with you, but your subscription can be restored with Restore Purchases.

Will passing the practice tests and mock exams make me pass the real exam?

No one can promise that. FrostPass is a study aid. The readiness score is an estimate based on your own answers in the app. Use it to find weak areas, and always check current regulations and your certifying organization's guidance.

Is FrostPass connected to the EPA?

No. FrostPass is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency or any certifying organization, and it does not issue certifications.
